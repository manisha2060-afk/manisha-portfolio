What I take off your hands

Pick the pieces you need. Most clients start with a calendar and coordination, then add reporting or audits as things settle. I plan, write, and coordinate the content; for design, video, or photography, I brief your designer or videographer rather than producing those assets myself.

Content calendars

A planned, on-brand posting schedule built around your goals and platforms, so you're not deciding what to post at 9pm the night before.

Captions & reel strategy

Captions written in your brand's voice, plus reel concepts and direction so your team knows exactly what to shoot.

Designer & vendor coordination

Briefing designers, videographers, and other vendors, and chasing assets so nothing holds up the week's posting.

Community management

Comments and DMs monitored and answered in your voice, so the account stays responsive without you checking it constantly.

Social media audits

A one-time review of what's working, what isn't, and where your accounts are leaving engagement on the table.

Reporting

A plain-language monthly summary of what went out and how it performed, without you needing to read a dashboard.
